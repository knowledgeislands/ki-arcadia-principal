# Techne Programme Hold

## Authority

Arcadia maintains the programme hold for Techné's engineering discipline, its retained source work, and the independent harness and CLI products. The principal named in the hold is the accountable human owner; relocation of knowledge does not change that person's decision authority.

## Holding position

Techné programme execution is on hold while the local Paperclip delivery model is established and its lessons are reviewed. This covers new implementation, substantive architecture changes, branch integration, remote-agent and infrastructure rollout across `ki-techne-principal`, `ki-techne-harness` and `tools-techne`, including work coordinated from another project. Existing task approvals and Ready records do not lift this hold.

Preserve existing branches, worktrees, commits, work records and services. Read-only inspection and explicitly scoped preservation or hold administration may continue; this is not cancellation, acceptance, disposal or a request to stop running infrastructure. The retained `TECHNE-OPS-002` candidates `0f77071572aa649a936be3069f635ab8ea721858` and `eb7292a1f1bd515fcb7c44715caac48bfe5770ad` remain reviewable work, not accepted changes to `main`.

Before proposing resumption, Convenor must bring back evidence of the local review-to-live-main cycle and recovery of accumulated output; a review of what Paperclip already supplies and what Techné still needs to add; and a repository-owned remote-delivery policy covering destination, visibility, review, integration, synchronisation and recovery. The `ki-agent-coordination-paperclip` skill owns the reusable policy boundary. The principal explicitly decides whether to resume, reshape or retire the existing work; passing checks alone does not resume it.

## Knowledge-owner transition

The approved [[KI-ARCADIA-ECO-007-consolidate-techne-knowledge|knowledge consolidation]] transfers canonical engineering knowledge to [[Engineering Practice/Engineering Practice|Engineering Practice]] while retaining source evidence and held work. The human's exception to the retiring source's Enactment prerequisite permits the scoped documentary authority transition only. It does not resume programme execution, accept candidates, change source work records or permit infrastructure action.

Source snapshots remain readable in `knowledgeislands/ki-techne-principal`, and the existing work records, ledger, candidates and worktrees remain in place.
