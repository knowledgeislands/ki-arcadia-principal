# Admin

## Overview

`Admin` is the governance and operations zone - one of the five canonical Knowledge Islands zones (`Calendar`, `Pillars`, `Resources`, `Streams`, `Admin`). It holds everything that defines what this island is and how it runs, migrated from Knowledge Capital per [[Admin/Governance/Decisions/GDR-KI-ARCADIA-002-admin-zone-governance-and-operations|GDR-KI-ARCADIA-002]]. The zone splits into two arms: Governance holds the artefacts that must be true for the island to be what it is, and Operations holds the artefacts that describe how it runs from day to day. A root memory index sits beside them as the entry point for a session. Changes to anything under `Admin` pass through the [[Admin/Operations/Processes/Enactment Process|Enactment Process]].

## Governance

[[Admin/Governance/Governance|Governance]] is the arm that defines what Arcadia is: its Charter, its Known Lands inventory, its conventions, its policies, its note templates and its Decision Records. Agents starting cold read the Charter here first, because it carries the island's identity parameters and its adoption position on every activity group. Nothing in this arm describes routine running; it records the rules and declarations that the running must respect.

## MEMORY

[[Admin/MEMORY|MEMORY]] is the root memory index named by the root `CLAUDE.md`. It lists the active zones and Pillars - Governance, Operations, the portable Knowledge Islands model and Engineering Practice - so that a session can scope itself before substantive work. It also signposts the Techne Programme Hold, the standing constraint every engineering session must read first.

## Operations

[[Admin/Operations/Operations|Operations]] is the arm that describes how Arcadia runs: its Activity notes and their timing model, its governance processes, its live artifacts, its installed skills and its register of operational mistakes and lessons. Its contents are operational and mutable, specific to Arcadia's own integrations and conventions. Where Governance says what must hold, Operations shows how the island keeps it holding.
