# Resources

## Overview

Resources holds external reference: material that exists independently of Knowledge Islands and is captured here because the island draws on it. Internal methodology and the island's own adaptations belong in Pillars; a note here describes its subject as its authors present it and points to where the island uses it. Each subject has its own folder.

---

## Productivity

[[Productivity]] holds reference on personal methods for capturing, organising and acting on work. It currently describes the Bullet Journal method, which the island's Calendar templates adapt for rapid logging and migration. Read it alongside the Calendar passage of [[Structure]] to see the source method and the island's adaptation side by side.
