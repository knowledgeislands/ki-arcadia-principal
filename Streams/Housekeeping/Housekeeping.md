# Housekeeping

## Overview

Housekeeping holds recurring-work templates. A template creates a linked, ordinary roadmap run when due; it is not a queue or a delivery-state folder.

## Current templates

No recurring templates were retained from the legacy Streams migration. Introduce a template here only when a recurring obligation, cadence, and successful-run evidence are explicitly defined.
