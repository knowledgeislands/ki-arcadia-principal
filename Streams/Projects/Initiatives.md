# Initiatives

## Overview

An Initiative is a long-lived direction for the Knowledge Islands territory. It never finishes: it is the grouping of the finite Projects that serve it, plus any upkeep that serves it without a Project. Initiatives have no notes of their own, so this index describes each one, names its Projects, and lists its projectless upkeep records. A work record stores its `project`, from which its Initiative is derived; upkeep and recurring Activities declare `initiative` directly once the migration tags them.

Membership is classification, not authority. Each owning repository decides whether its records join when the migration tags them, and status lives in each record, not here. Record lists below are from the theme map of 2026-10-07, refreshed against current record status, with done records omitted.

## Platform foundations

Slug `platform-foundations`. The shared base every island stands on: the `ki` toolchain and its baseline, the harness standards, and the factorisation of the estate into clearly owned repositories. Its Projects are [[baseline-rollout]], which gets every island to a green estate audit on the released `ki`, and [[Projects/estate-factorisation|estate-factorisation]], which finishes the ownership, MCP and distribution work.

## Knowledge Islands model

Slug `knowledge-islands-model`. What a Knowledge Island is and how islands work together: the model and its tending practice, trade between islands, how outside knowledge comes in, and how the model is specified and presented. Its active Projects are [[island-model-and-tending]] and [[knowledge-acquisition]]; [[Projects/territories-and-trades|territories-and-trades]] is planned; [[specifications]] and [[website]] are paused, each with a finite outcome to resume against.
