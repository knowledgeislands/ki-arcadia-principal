# Design

## Overview

This folder holds the supporting notes for the [[paperclip-capture-and-rebuild|Paperclip capture and rebuild]] Project. They record working context the Project note points to but should not carry itself. Like other design papers, they are temporary: once the rebuild settles their content in a durable home, they are deleted in one commit.

## Rebuild structure

[[paperclip-rebuild-structure]] records the structure of the `terra` Paperclip instance when it was stopped on 2026-10-14: companies and codes, projects per repository, agents with their roles and remits, routines and the common adapter settings, with what to keep and what to change in a fresh rebuild. It holds no secrets and no issue content.
