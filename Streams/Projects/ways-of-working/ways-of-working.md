# Ways of Working

## Outcome

Define how Kris works with agents - one master thread for project management, a small number of Project threads, detached background agents and the `ki` tools - in skills that transfer to any repository and runtime, rather than in personal preferences. The test: the thread and delegation rules live in `ki-delegation` and its sibling skills, a thread can roll up what has happened since a mark, and Kris can see the state of play across Projects and Initiatives at a glance.

This Project sits in [[knowledge-islands-model|Knowledge Islands model]].

---

- The thread working rules already in `ki-delegation` - parking tangents, linking records, keeping primary checkouts current, reporting to the owner and refreshing the guidance - are this Project's to refine.
- A clear state-of-play view may draw on apps-observatory.local and the command-centre ideas in hnr-principal and legal-principal.

It suggests a rename that says iteration and project governance, a check on whether `change-management` is still its area, and batching the rename (about 38 references in 17 harness files, plus installed payloads and the catalogue) with any other harness renames, routed to the harness as a trade.
- The change-management design is in progress under `ki-pmo-design` (named `ki-design-loop` until 14 October 2026) in this Project's [[ways-of-working/design/design|design folder]]: change management as a whole, the `ki-pmo` thread skill, checkpoints and the mark, goals at every level, the `ki` command surface and the default coordinator model (Decisions 4 to 8 of the ways-of-working run).
