# Roadmap

## Overview

Roadmap records are Arcadia's finite, forward work. Each record is flat in this folder; its identifier, horizon, lifecycle status, priority, dependencies, and current planning detail are held in the record itself. The allocation and owner-reviewed legacy migration are recorded in [[Streams/Roadmap/_ISSUES|the issue ledger]].

## Areas

`ECO` covers cross-estate coordination and work with no natural repository owner. Arcadia owns the question, participating set, routing, evidence, and estate roll-up; receiving repositories retain implementation, release, and acceptance. `GOV` covers Arcadia governance and operating-model changes. `MOD` covers the Knowledge Islands model and its canonical concepts. `OPS` covers Arcadia's tools, automation, and operating practice. `EXT` covers work that adopts or extracts the model across other islands and the wider ecosystem.

## Using the roadmap

Select, shape, deliver, review, and close roadmap work through the shared change-management lifecycle. Horizon is metadata - it is not represented by another folder. Completed work remains a `done` record until its owner explicitly selects it for pruning.
