# Establish estate factorisation contract

## Goal

Re-verify the evidence underpinning the Knowledge Islands factorisation roadmap, establish its repository-structure taxonomy, and prepare the exact in-place amendment and delivery boundary for `GDR-KI-FUNDAMENTALS-001`.

## Context

[The consolidated factorisation roadmap](../../+/knowledge-islands-factorisation-roadmap.md) makes Arcadia Principal the home of shared estate initiatives. The source estate changed during review, so the work first established a dated evidence baseline and then prepared the present-state fundamentals amendment.

## Boundary

This item covers read-only estate verification, structure and responsibility analysis, correction of the coordination sources, and preparation of the approved successor implementation records. It does not amend the shared Decision Record, modify another repository, create estate-wide specifications, merge OpenAI MCP repositories, or issue every repository's later alignment item.

## Current state

The current recheck records 24 repositories before the OpenAI merge and 23 after it, including governed `tools-techne` and `apps-observatory`. Project and Knowledge Base are the only base structures; structural overlays, adapters, estate roles, projections, and runtime ownership remain separate dimensions.

[The fundamentals amendment plan](../../+/knowledge-islands-fundamentals-amendment-plan.md) now contains the exact approved responsibility and routing contract. `KI-ARCADIA-ECO-001` owns its six-copy implementation. `KI-ARCADIA-ECO-002` owns the direct `ki-all`, `ki-fnd`, and `ki-tools` move; OAI-1 retains the later `ki-mcps` move.
