# Streams

## Overview

Streams are Arcadia's operational container for knowledge in motion. Finite forward work lives flat in [[Roadmap]], while recurring obligations are defined once as Activity notes in the configured collection, currently [[Admin/Operations/Activities/Activities|Admin Activities]]; when an opted-in housekeeping Activity falls due it creates a linked ordinary roadmap record, so Streams holds no separate housekeeping area. Durable knowledge belongs in `Admin/`, `Pillars/`, or `Resources/`, not in Streams.

---

## Roadmap

[[Roadmap]] contains the flat work records and [[Streams/Roadmap/_ISSUES|allocation ledger]]. Each record carries its own horizon, status, priority, dependencies, and current delivery detail; it never moves between priority folders.
