# Streams

## Overview

Streams are Arcadia's operational container for knowledge in motion. Finite forward work lives flat in [[Roadmap]], while recurring obligations live as templates in [[Housekeeping]]. Durable knowledge belongs in `Admin/`, `Pillars/`, or `Resources/`, not in Streams.

---

## Roadmap

[[Roadmap]] contains the flat work records and [[Streams/Roadmap/_ISSUES|allocation ledger]]. Each record carries its own horizon, status, priority, dependencies, and current delivery detail; it never moves between priority folders.

## Housekeeping

[[Housekeeping]] contains recurring-work templates. When a template is due, it spawns a linked ordinary roadmap record that follows the shared lifecycle.
