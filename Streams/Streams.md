# Streams

## Overview

Streams are Knowledge Islands Principal's operational container for knowledge in motion. Finite forward work lives flat in [[Roadmap]], while recurring obligations are defined once as Activity notes in the configured collection, currently [[Admin/Operations/Activities/Activities|Admin Activities]]; when an opted-in housekeeping Activity falls due it creates a linked ordinary roadmap record, so Streams holds no separate housekeeping area. Durable knowledge belongs in `Admin/`, `Pillars/`, or `Resources/`, not in Streams.

---

## Roadmap

[[Roadmap]] contains the flat work records and [[Streams/Roadmap/_ISSUES|allocation ledger]]. Each record carries its own horizon, status, priority, dependencies, and current delivery detail; it never moves between priority folders.

---

## Projects

[[Projects]] is the territory's Project registry: one note per finite Project, named by its slug, carrying its outcome, lead, target and `lifecycle`, with an Outcome section and Notes for ideas and context. An idea-stage Project is a `planned` one whose outcome is a decision its lead takes. Links point upwards: each work record names its Project, so a Project note lists no records and carries no health or update section; status lives in each record.

---

## Initiatives

[[Initiatives]] holds one note per long-lived Initiative, named by its slug, which its Projects and projectless records name. Each note gives its direction and Notes for ideas, context and the projectless upkeep it carries; it lists neither its Projects nor their records or Activities, and carries no dated review or status section.
