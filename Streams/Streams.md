# Streams

## Overview

Streams are projects and workstreams organised by their current level of focus. Every piece of work in progress - or ever concluded - lives here. Streams move between sub-folders as priority changes; when a stream concludes, it is moved to Future and may at a later date be removed and its notes subsumed into pillars.

## Now

[[Now]] streams are receiving focused attention now - the work is happening. Now is not a queue; it should hold only what is genuinely in motion. When a stream stalls or loses urgency, it moves to Soon or Parked.

---

## Soon

[[Soon]] streams are alive but not the current focus. They surface periodically, accumulate thinking, and may graduate to Now when circumstances change. A stream in Soon is not forgotten - it is incubating.

---

## Parked

[[Parked]] streams are paused with intention to return. Something specific blocked or deprioritised them - not abandoned, but not being worked. The distinction from Soon is intent: Parked means "we will return when the blocker clears"; Soon means "we are working on it, just not urgently".

---

## Future

[[Future]] holds ideas and planned work that has not yet started, plus completed streams retained until their evidence can be explicitly pruned. Draft entries are candidates for promotion to Now or Soon when capacity or priority aligns. Done entries remain only as retained history after their knowledge has been extracted to the appropriate Pillars notes.
