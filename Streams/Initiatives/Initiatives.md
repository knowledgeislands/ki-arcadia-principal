# Initiatives

## Overview

This folder holds the Knowledge Islands territory's Initiatives, the long-lived directions its work serves, held by Arcadia as Capital beside the [[Projects]] they group. An Initiative never finishes. Each note is named by its slug, so a work record's or Activity's `initiative` value resolves to a path here, and carries the direction, the Projects that serve it, its projectless upkeep, its recurring Activities and a dated Review.

The Review section of each note is the territory's periodic judgement of that direction: how its Projects stand, the decisions it needs and one next step. It replaces the per-theme checkpoints and the cross-theme state-of-play review. Membership is classification, not authority: each owning repository keeps its plan, priority and acceptance, and status lives only in each record.

---

## Platform foundations

[[platform-foundations]] is the shared base every island stands on: the `ki` toolchain and its baseline, the harness standards, and the factorisation of the estate into clearly owned repositories. Its Projects are the baseline rollout, estate factorisation and the roadmap model. It also carries the standards upkeep that keeps the harness and `ki` accurate.

---

## Techne

[[Initiatives/techne|Techne]] moves agent work into a governed Techne footprint and makes the agent fleet useful, within the Techne Programme Hold. Its Projects are the agent host, Paperclip bootstrap and recovery, and the Delta evaluation. It has no projectless upkeep.

---

## Knowledge Islands model

[[knowledge-islands-model]] is what a Knowledge Island is and how islands work together: the model and its tending, trade between islands, how outside knowledge comes in, and how the model is specified and presented. It has five Projects, two of them paused, and owns Arcadia's recurring Tending and Briefings Activities.

---

## Rig

[[rig]] is the workstation every agent and person runs on: chezmoi-managed configuration, local services and their hygiene. It has no Projects; its work is workstation-hygiene upkeep and chezmoi's housekeeping templates.
