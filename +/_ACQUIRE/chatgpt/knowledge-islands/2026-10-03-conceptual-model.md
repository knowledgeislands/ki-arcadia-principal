## Realm

A Realm is a logical environment with its own rules of existence and participation.

A Realm can define:

- identity and authentication;
- permissions and authorisation;
- capabilities an Avatar may exercise;
- what knowledge or capabilities may cross its boundary;
- organisations and territories within it;
- security constraints;
- history and state through time.

The initial concrete Realm is the **Knowledge Islands Realm**.

### Avatar names per Realm

The same underlying human may use different Avatar names or manifestations in different Realms.

Open point (2026-10-11): ADR-KI-ARCADIA-005 decides one enduring persona per person across explicit working contexts, with Kitteth as Kris's instance. Whether a persona may carry a different Avatar name or manifestation per Realm is not settled there.

## Territories, islands and organisations

Personal and organisational islands can exist within the wider Knowledge Islands Realm. Organisations can be treated as territories or governed spaces in which an Avatar acts under particular permissions.

The model should support movement between such spaces without requiring identity and authority to be identical everywhere.

## Knowledge landscapes

Knowledge Landscapes is a related concept describing structure, terrain, navigability and potentially the kinds of activity possible within knowledge space.

The exact relationship between **Island**, **Landscape**, and **Realm** remains intentionally open. A useful current distinction is:

- Island: bounded body/domain of knowledge or civilisation unit.
- Landscape: structure and navigable terrain of knowledge.
- Realm: governed environment in which entities exist and interact.

These are conceptual roles, not final definitions.

## Time and state

A place is not only a location; it has history.

The architecture may eventually allow knowledge environments to be viewed or traversed at different points in time, analogous to moving between states, memories or historical versions of a Realm.

This is a long-horizon concept rather than a requirement for the first prototype.

## Knowledge Realms registry/portal

A future **Knowledge Realms** capability could provide a way for Realms to declare or register their existence and define how Avatars may enter and interoperate.

Security, trust, authorisation and capability negotiation would be fundamental. Entry into another Realm should not imply unrestricted transfer of local capabilities or authority.
