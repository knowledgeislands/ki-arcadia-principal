# Ancient civilizations insights

## Attendees

Kris Brown (note creator) from Humans Not Robots <kris@humansnotrobots.com>

## Ancient Civilizations and Monuments

- Fascination with a time-lapse visualization of how civilizations emerged: Mesopotamia, Göbekli Tepe, Stonehenge, Machu Picchu, Aztec temples, pyramids
- Seven Wonders as a connective thread across cultures and eras
- Colossus of Rhodes as one example: one of the Seven Wonders, the giant warrior guarding the gates
- Debate on whether ancient monument-building reflects shared human ingenuity or something more exotic (extraterrestrial influence floated)

## Ice Ages, Geology, and Lost Worlds

- Sea levels much lower during ice sheet periods
- Mediterranean likely filled catastrophically as ice melted: salt flats, evaporated lakes, then refilling via Gibraltar
  - Most plausible location for Atlantis, per the discussion
- Magnetic polarity shifts recorded in geological deposits: happened multiple times, potentially catastrophic in effect
- Sediment records give a lot of data, but the _why_ behind ice ages remains unclear

## Viking Reach and Revisionism

- Vikings traveled far beyond Scandinavia: Iceland, Canada, Gibraltar, Cyprus, Constantinople, Mediterranean
  - Got stranded on Iceland due to lack of trees for ship repair
- Reputation as purely violent raiders challenged
  - Early raids (e.g. Lindisfarne) likely rogue actors, not representative
  - When larger groups settled, they were largely civilized
  - Saxons, by contrast, massacred Viking settlers under the Danelaw
- Great Heathen Army (~AD 864): traveled the Humber, Ouse to York, Trent to Derby, using river systems extensively

## Vikings, Normans, and 1066

- Dukes of Normandy were Viking descendants, given the region by the Gauls to stop further raids
- 1066 reframed: not Saxon vs. Norman, but Viking vs. Viking
  - Harold Hardrada: Viking descent
  - Normans (William I): Viking descent via Rollo of Normandy
  - Rollo likely the same figure as “Vola,” great-great-great-grandfather of William the First, matching the Ragnar Lothbrok saga timeline
- Stamford Bridge and Hastings both within 200 years of peak Viking movement: still living history

## Mythology Grounded in History

- Greek and Norse mythology increasingly traceable to real events and places
  - Troy and the Trojan War: real archaeological basis
  - Mount Olympus on Cyprus: possible real-world anchor for Greek gods (Aphrodite, etc.)
- Ragnar Lothbrok saga: names descendants including Ivar the Boneless, all part of the Great Heathen Army
- Pattern across cultures: mythology often encodes real battles, migrations, and geography
