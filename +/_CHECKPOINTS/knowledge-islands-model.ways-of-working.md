- Report heading: in chat, three `---` rules with blank lines between them, then `# [Knowledge Islands model](<Initiative note>) › [Ways of working](<Project note>)`, both linked by absolute path; a blank line; one bold line `**<Form> at <local time> · last mark <local time>**`, the form being Summary, Recap, Full summary or Re-bootstrap; a blank line and one `---`. A saved report file omits the three opening rules so they are not read as front matter. No frame, thread type, window or state opens a report.
- Sections, in this order and every time: `## Done`, `## In Progress` with "Outstanding - no action requested now" beneath it, `## Queries`. An empty section still appears and says so. A full summary adds `## Decided` first.
- Queries: one `### <TAG> - <title>` per actionable query, the tag a stable Needs tag linked to its record where one exists, carrying bold **Context**, **Query** and **Options** only; the options are lettered lines with no bullet, `a) (recommended)` always first. Then one fenced `text` reply block, stacked: the tag alone on its line, a blank line, then option a)'s instruction written out in full, entries separated by a blank line, `---` and a blank line. A bare letter is accepted as the answer and is expanded by hand until `ki agent decide` resolves it.
- Footer: the padded block-letter frame, 52 code points per line, top border `╭─ <form> · <UTC> ─` filled to width, one blank framed line, the three REST or BUSY rows with their captions, one blank framed line, the bottom border. Then a blank line and the `- [state]` list: `[at rest]` only when nothing runs or queues, else `[running]`, `[queued]`, `[waiting] <TAG>`, then `[committed]`, `[uncommitted]`, `[unpushed]` or `[blocked]`, `[held]`, `[parked]`, `[recovered]` and `[unlogged]`. Last comes the resume line in its own one-line fenced `text` block. Nothing in the banner cites a decision number.
- Forms: a full Summary only when all dispatched work has finished or Kris asks; otherwise timed one-to-two-line updates while work runs, with a heartbeat wake every 5 minutes, as Kris set with "HEARTBEAT - while work runs, the thread's background check also wakes it every 5 minutes for a one-to-two-line timed update with each agent's current step; add it to the ki-pmo update cadence and the hook line, and apply it in this thread now". When all active work concludes, the rest summary is given again in full with every open query and the reply block. A recap covers the window since the mark; only Kris moves the mark.

Every owner decision is logged in Kris's words in the turn it arrives, before the work it authorises is launched; decisions are described by their words and date, never by number.
