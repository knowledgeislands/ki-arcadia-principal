- **Price and stock:** £29.95 on the product page, a limited-time deal (RRP £39.99, -25%). In stock, Amazon's Choice, "2K+ bought in past month".
- **Rating:** 4.5 out of 5 from 2,521 ratings. Amazon's summary praises picture quality, value and plug-and-play setup on Mac and Windows. Some buyers say the auto light correction could be better.
- **Sensor:** 8 MP CMOS, 4K30, f/1.8, 70° field of view, autofocus (stepper motor and IR rangefinder). The sensor size is not published. It is almost certainly smaller than the 1/2-inch sensors on the shortlist.
- **Microphones:** "dual noise-canceling mics", which the listing says filter out keyboard clicks and background noise. There is no beamforming and no mode selection. Of 15 buyers who mention sound quality, 13 are positive. Their comments include "sound quality is good, even in my very echo-y apartment", "clear and adequate for conversation" and "voices come through clear and natural". No independent review or audio test turned up, so this rests on buyer reviews alone.
- **Connector:** fixed USB-C cable that cannot be swapped out, with a USB-C to USB-A adapter in the box. One buyer says the adapters on both cameras they bought failed. That doesn't matter on sol, which has USB-C ports.
- **Privacy cover:** detachable flip cover in the box.
- **Mounting:** monitor clip. One buyer says it is not very secure, and another mentions a tripod option. The page does not confirm a tripod thread.
- **macOS:** listed for Mac OS X 10.6 and later. Several buyers say it works straight away on a Mac. macOS 27 is not mentioned, but the camera is plain UVC/UAC with no driver, which is how the shortlist judges every camera.
- **App:** none. It has no control app, so there is nothing to install through Homebrew or the Mac App Store, and image settings go through the calling app.

- **How it stacks up:**
  - **Against the Link 2C Pro (£199):** it is a sixth of the price, but it has a smaller and unnamed sensor and no beamforming or Focus mode, and no reviewer has tested its audio. Those mics are what the recommendation rests on for dictation.
  - **Against the Tiny 3 Lite (£169):** the Tiny 3 Lite has three mics with directional modes and a 1/2-inch sensor. Here the UGREEN 4K is again ahead only on price.
  - **Against the FineCam Pro (£57.78):** it is the FineCam Pro's simpler sibling, with a smaller sensor, no gesture framing and the same thin audio evidence. Its ratings are far better, though: 4.5 from 2,521 against 3.8 from 334. For half the price, it is the safer budget choice of the two UGREEN cameras.
- **Verdict: the recommendation does not change.** The Link 2C Pro is still the pick, with the Tiny 3 Lite as the cheaper alternative. The UGREEN 4K is a good, well-rated £30 calls camera. It would replace the FineCam Pro as the budget option. Because nobody has tested its mics, it is not the camera to rely on for dictation.
