### Microphone (USB-C, class-compliant on macOS)

All four work on a Mac without vendor software. The Shure and RØDE apps are optional.

| Product | Price, 15 Oct 2026 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [Shure MV7+ (black, mic only)](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0CTJ7PVN1) | £255.00, sold by Amazon | **Recommended.** A dynamic mic that rejects room noise, which suits dictation. It has USB-C and XLR, so it stays useful if you upgrade later. Auto Level and the denoiser run on the mic itself. No stand in the box, so pair it with the arm below. |
| [RØDE PSA1+ boom arm](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B09JBVR5B4) | £85.00 | **Recommended with the MV7+.** A dynamic mic needs to sit close to your mouth, and the arm keeps it there without cluttering the desk. Rated 4.7 from 17k reviews. |
| [Shure MV6 Gen 2](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0HD9VYLY7) | £160.21 | **Cheaper pick.** Same Shure voice processing in a smaller body, with a stand and cable in the box. New this month, so it has only 2 ratings. The [Gen 1](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0DBJ5DBL8) is £131.20 but takes 9 to 10 weeks to dispatch. |
| [RØDE PodMic USB](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0BQM4TKF7) | £155.00, sold by Amazon | A strong alternative: a dynamic mic with USB and XLR. It's heavy, so it needs an arm. Rated 4.2. |
| [RØDE NT-USB Mini](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B084P1CXFD) | offers from about £66 to £90 | The cheapest option, with a stand included. It's a condenser mic, so it picks up more room and keyboard noise than the dynamic mics. That makes it the weakest choice for dictation. |

### Speakers (wired input, not Bluetooth only)

sol has a 3.5 mm headphone jack and USB-C, but no optical. A USB input is cleanest: the speakers become their own audio device in macOS, with a digital signal path and no hum from an analogue lead. Speakers with only analogue inputs connect from the 3.5 mm jack with a 3.5 mm to dual-RCA lead.

| Product | Price, 15 Oct 2026 | Inputs that suit sol | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Kanto ORA (black)](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0CLHKBKL8) | £249.00 (5 left; another snapshot showed £295.50) | USB-C (also RCA and Bluetooth) | **Recommended.** Compact, 50 W RMS, with strong bass for their size. One USB-C cable to sol. If you add a subwoofer later, the sub output switches on a crossover automatically, so these won't need replacing. Few UK ratings so far (4.2 from 17). |
| [Audioengine A2+ Wireless (black)](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B07MYV2527) | £248.00 | USB, 3.5 mm or RCA (also Bluetooth) | A well-proven alternative at the same price, rated 4.6 from about 3,000 reviews. 60 W peak. |
| [Edifier S880DB MKII (walnut)](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0FGNS3KFC) | £234.91 (Prime price; £335.59 without Prime) | USB-C or RCA (also optical, coax and Bluetooth) | Bigger bookshelf speakers at 88 W RMS, with a sub output. Good value with Prime, but they take more desk space. |
