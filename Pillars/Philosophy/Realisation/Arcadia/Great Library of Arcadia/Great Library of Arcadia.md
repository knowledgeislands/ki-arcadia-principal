# Great Library of Arcadia

## Overview

The Great Library of Arcadia is a philosophical idea, not a structure. It explains what Arcadia is for within Knowledge Islands; it does not prescribe how Arcadia's knowledge base, or any other, is laid out. This note closes [[Realisation/Realisation|Realisation]] by naming the role the earlier acts have been building towards.

---

## A library of libraries

In the model, a library is a source of knowledge. [[The Home of Knowledge]] gives every island its Library: the canonical, version-controlled record that changes only through governance. Each island's Library is its own, and each serves the people and agents who work there.

Arcadia's library is different in kind rather than in size. As the Capital and the home of the model, Arcadia holds the idea of what a library is and how one is kept, and it knows where the other libraries are. It is effectively the library of all libraries - and that is why it is "great".

---

## An echo of Alexandria

The name is a deliberate play on the Great Library of Alexandria. [[History of Knowledge Systems]] recalls Alexandria, the House of Wisdom in Baghdad and Ashurbanipal's library at Nineveh as the first attempts to gather everything known under one roof, and the single point of failure that came with that ambition. [[Layers of Knowledge]] places libraries in the civilisational layer, where knowledge outlives any single mind.

The Great Library of Arcadia keeps the ambition and declines the method. It does not gather every island's knowledge into one place. Knowledge stays in the island that owns it, and Arcadia's greatness lies in holding the model that every island's library shares and in pointing to where the rest is kept.

---

## A reference, not a jurisdiction

Being the library of libraries confers no authority over them. [[Jurisdiction]] gives every island authority over what reaches its own Library, and [[SDR-KI-ARCADIA-005-territories-archipelagos-and-the-constitutional-layer|SDR-KI-ARCADIA-005]] makes Arcadia the canonical home of the public model while denying it any universal meta-jurisdiction. Another territory chooses whether to reference or adopt that knowledge, records its source and revision, and owns its local adaptation.

Within the Knowledge Islands territory, [[Known Lands]] owns the inventory of member islands and signposts external lands. Contributions to the model flow back to Arcadia only by consent, through its own governance, and the [[Arcadia]] website publishes from the library without becoming a third source of truth.
