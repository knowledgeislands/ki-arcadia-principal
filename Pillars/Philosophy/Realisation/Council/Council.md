# Council

## Overview

The Council is the governing body of a Knowledge Island and the second constitutionally required element of realisation, alongside the [[Realisation/Charter/Charter|Charter]]. [[SDR-KI-ARCADIA-003-the-governance-of-an-island|SDR-KI-ARCADIA-003]] defines it as the body that ratifies proposals and holds authority on the island, and declares that each island's `Admin/` zone declares its council. Its authority is not exercised separately from the island's processes: the [[Processes/Enactment Process/Enactment Process|Enactment Process]] is how the council operates. This note describes the council at model level; Arcadia's own council is declared in its Enactment Process.

---

## What the Council Decides

The council decides what becomes stable knowledge. Nothing reaches `Pillars`, `Resources` or the governed parts of `Admin` except through a proposal the council has ratified. For each proposal the council can **ratify** it, so that it proceeds to rollout; **return** it to draft for further work, with reasons; or **reject** it as a terminal outcome, with reasons recorded. Its members are the island's custodians in the [[Glossary]]'s sense: the people who know the island well enough to accept change on its behalf.

---

## Standing and Eligibility

Every person who interacts with an island holds a standing within it, defined in [[Jurisdiction]]:

- A **Visitor** may contribute informally but cannot make formal proposals or sit on the council.
- A **Citizen** has full standing and is eligible for the council, but formal proposals still go through a council member.
- A **Council Member** may make formal proposals through the Enactment Process and ratify them.

Citizenship is a prerequisite for council membership, and the council - or the sole governor of a single-governor island - grants it. A person may hold citizenship on several islands and so be eligible for several councils. A Visitor with a substantive contribution may seek citizenship or ask a council member to carry the proposal forward.

---

## Governance Patterns

The council is one of several governance patterns in [[Jurisdiction]]. A formal council suits collaborative or multi-governor islands and framework custodians; joint governors may share authority without a council structure; and a single-person island has one governor. On a single-person island the sole member stands in for the council: the formality is light, but the record of who holds that authority is still kept.

---

## Where the Council Is Recorded

The council's membership is island-specific realisation and lives in `Admin/`, not in the portable model. Arcadia is a single-person island: its [[Admin/Operations/Processes/Enactment Process|Enactment Process]] names the island owner as sole Council member and approver. An explicit owner instruction for a bounded change stands in for a work record, so the council's authority is exercised either through a record that reaches review or through that direct instruction.
