# Model

## Overview

Model is the second act of the Knowledge Islands pillar, following [[Introduction/Introduction|Introduction]] and preceding [[Realisation/Realisation|Realisation]]. Where the Introduction explains why the model exists and establishes its vocabulary, the Model sets out what any island adopting Knowledge Islands actually does: the conventions it writes in, the processes by which it changes, the activities that keep it alive, the agents that act on it, and the tools those agents use. Everything here is portable and island-agnostic; island-specific settings and instances belong in `Admin/` or in the Realisation act.

The five chapters build on one another. Conventions define the shared language, Processes govern how that language and the content written in it change, Activities keep the island healthy between changes, Agents describe who carries the work out, and Tools describe what they connect through. Each chapter opens with a narrative chapter note, then holds the detailed notes in sub-folders.

---

## Conventions

[[Model/Conventions/Conventions|Conventions]] is the island's shared language: what a note looks like, where it lives, and which island it belongs to. It covers note format and metadata, the zone and folder structure, and residency across islands. Read it first, because every later chapter assumes its vocabulary.

---

## Processes

[[Model/Processes/Processes|Processes]] holds the mechanisms by which governance is enacted in practice. The Enactment Process governs how an island moves a proposal to ratified canonical content; the Contribution Process governs how knowledge developed elsewhere is proposed back into the shared model. Together they explain why nothing reaches a canonical zone by accident.

---

## Activities

[[Model/Activities/Activities|Activities]] describes the recurring work that keeps an island accurate and free of drift, divided into constitutional activities every island must run and adoptable groups each territory takes an explicit position on. It also carries the authoring guidelines for writing activity notes and their prompts. This chapter explains what each activity does and why; the runnable Claude prompts live under Tools.

---

## Agents

[[Model/Agents/Agents|Agents]] covers who acts on the island and how: human curation alongside agentic AI operation. It documents the operating conventions, permissions and behavioural patterns for each kind of agent. It pairs with Tools, which documents how the same systems are configured and connected rather than how they behave.

---

## Tools

[[Model/Tools/Tools|Tools]] documents the tools and services agents use to work with the island, mostly connected through MCP servers. Each tool has its own folder covering connection, operating conventions and lessons learned, while island-specific identifiers stay in Admin integration notes. It is the most practical chapter and the one most likely to change as the tool estate evolves.
