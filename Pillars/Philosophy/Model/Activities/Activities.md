# Activities

## Overview

Activities are the recurring work that keeps an island accurate and coherent over time. Within the Model act, this chapter defines what each activity does and why it exists, independent of any particular agent or island. Without them, an island drifts from the world it reflects: notes go stale, links break, and captured material never reaches its permanent home.

The chapter is organised by activity group. One group is constitutional and binds every island; the rest are adoptable, and each island records its position on them in its Charter. Alongside the groups sits the guidance for designing and writing activities. The Claude-specific prompts that run these activities live separately in [[Claude/Activities/Activities|Tools/Claude/Activities]].

Start with [[What Keeps an Island Alive]], the narrative chapter note, before moving into the individual groups.

---

## What Keeps an Island Alive

[[What Keeps an Island Alive]] is the entry point to the chapter. It explains why routine maintenance matters, distinguishes scheduled from conversational activities, and sets out the split between constitutional and adoptable groups. It also gives a summary of every activity in each group, so a reader can see the whole maintenance cycle at a glance before following a link into the detail.

---

## Constitutional

[[Model/Activities/Constitutional/Constitutional|Constitutional]] holds the baseline every Knowledge Island must meet, which sits outside the adoption framework and cannot be vetoed. It explains why the Charter and the Conformance Check are the two constitutional elements, and why the mechanism that verifies compliance is itself constitutional. Read it to understand what separates a Knowledge Island from a repository that merely borrows the vocabulary.

---

## Tending

[[Model/Activities/Tending/Tending|Tending]] is the adoptable group that keeps the island structurally sound and free of stale or orphaned material. It covers the scheduled automations that run without prompting and the conversational reviews triggered on demand, from inbox clearance to deep structural audits. Each activity has its own note in the folder, and the index closes with the adoption requirements an island must satisfy to adopt or veto the group.

---

## Authoring Guidelines

[[Authoring Guidelines]] is the framework for designing and maintaining activities across many islands and agents. It explains how an activity's design is split according to how general each element is across activity, island and agent, so that shared material is written once and reused. It also sets the contract for constitutional and adoptable groups, the required adoption requirements section, and a sequence for designing a new activity. Read it before writing or changing an activity note or its prompt.
