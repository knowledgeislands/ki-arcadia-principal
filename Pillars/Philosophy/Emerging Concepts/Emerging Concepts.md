# Emerging Concepts

## Overview

Emerging Concepts holds ideas about Knowledge Islands that are worth keeping but are not settled. Nothing here is adopted philosophy: where a note sits at odds with [[Introduction/Introduction|Introduction]], [[Model/Model|Model]], [[Realisation/Realisation|Realisation]] or a Decision Record, the established text governs. Each note says where it came from and which established notes it touches.

The folder exists so that open concepts live in the knowledge base proper, where they can be iterated on and linked, rather than in an acquisition inbox. Each is an input to the whole-philosophy review, which will decide whether to adopt, reshape or drop it. The first notes came from a ChatGPT conversation captured on 2026-10-03.

---

## Realm Model

[[Realm Model]] sketches the Realm: a logical environment with its own rules of identity, authority, capability and history, within which islands, organisations and Avatars act. It sets out the working distinction between Island, Landscape and Realm, the questions of Avatar portability and of travelling through a Realm's history, and a possible Knowledge Realms registry. It also records an open tension with the one-persona decision over Avatar names per Realm.

## Fictional Lineage

[[Fictional Lineage]] records the speculative fiction behind the model - _Tron_, _The Matrix_, _Otherland_ and others - and the specific idea each one contributed. It complements the real-world lineage already told in the Introduction's Background chapter. It closes with a principle for capturing future inspirations so that the record stays about ideas rather than becoming a reference list.

## Avatar Roles and Oversight

[[Avatar Roles and Oversight]] holds three open Techne questions about delegated agency: whether an Avatar may ever act as Operator or Architect, how events from delegated work reach the human, and what agent telemetry to capture. They stay open while the Techne Programme Hold keeps every Avatar held.
