# Avatar Roles and Oversight

## Overview

This is an emerging concept, not adopted philosophy. It holds three Techne questions about delegated agency that stay open while the [[Techne Programme Hold]] keeps Kitteth and every Avatar held. They belong to the reshaping of that hold and to the whole-philosophy review, not to current work.

---

## Operator and Architect

The Operator is an external, privileged operational role; the Architect shapes the rules and structure of an environment. The open question is whether these are separate roles, capability sets of the same Avatar, authorities reserved to the human, or modes that depend on context.

The current position is settled for the prototype. On the agent host, Kris acts as Operator and Architect through a role only a human assumes, and no agent creates access; the [[Agent Host Prototype Concept Map]] shows this. Whether an Avatar may ever hold either capability is what remains open.

---

## How events reach the human

Delegated work needs a defined path by which progress, completion, exceptions and requests for approval reach the human. Which events are surfaced, through which channel and with what urgency has not been decided; every messaging channel is currently held.

---

## Agent telemetry

Meaningful agent and workload telemetry should be captured so that delegated work can be observed and reviewed. What counts as meaningful, and where it is kept, is undecided.
