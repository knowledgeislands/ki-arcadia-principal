# Realm Model

## Overview

This is an emerging concept, not adopted philosophy. It sketches the Realm: a logical environment with its own rules of existence and participation, inside which islands, organisations and Avatars act. Nothing here changes the established model; it is held so it can be tested against real use and weighed in the whole-philosophy review.

Arcadia already holds one part of the idea. The [[Agent Host Prototype Concept Map]] treats a Realm as logical, never a technology, and keeps it apart from any Footprint. The rest of the model below is unsettled.

---

## What a Realm defines

A Realm could define:

- identity and authentication;
- permissions and authorisation;
- the capabilities an Avatar may exercise;
- what knowledge or capability may cross its boundary;
- the organisations and territories within it;
- its security constraints;
- its history and state through time.

The first concrete Realm would be the Knowledge Islands Realm.

---

## Islands and organisations within a Realm

Personal and organisational islands could exist within the wider Knowledge Islands Realm, and an organisation could be treated as a governed space in which an Avatar acts under particular permissions. The model should allow movement between such spaces without requiring identity and authority to be identical everywhere.

How this sits beside [[Territories and Archipelagos]] is open. There, a territory is a governed collection of islands under one accountable authority; whether an organisation inside a Realm is a territory, contains one, or is a separate notion has not been worked out.

---

## Island, Landscape and Realm

The working direction distinguishes three terms:

- **Island** - a bounded body or domain of knowledge, or a unit of civilisation;
- **Landscape** - the structure and navigable terrain of knowledge, possibly including the kinds of activity possible within it;
- **Realm** - the governed environment in which entities exist and interact, setting the rules of existence, identity, capability and interaction.

These are conceptual roles, not definitions. They should be tested against real use cases before any is fixed.

---

## Avatar names per Realm

The same person might use different Avatar names or manifestations in different Realms.

This sits in tension with [[ADR-KI-ARCADIA-005-one-persona-across-explicit-working-contexts|ADR-KI-ARCADIA-005]], which gives each person one enduring persona across explicit working contexts, with Kitteth as Kris's instance. Whether that one persona may carry a different name or manifestation per Realm is not settled; the decision stands until a review says otherwise.

---

## Avatar portability

What follows an Avatar between Realms is undefined: identity, selected knowledge, credentials, capabilities, history, reputation or trust, and active work are all candidates. Entry into a Realm should not grant capabilities held elsewhere.

---

## Time and state

A place is not only a location; it has a history. Knowledge environments might one day be viewed or traversed at different points in time, as one moves between states, memories or historical versions of a Realm. This is a long-horizon idea, not a requirement for any current prototype. Its open questions are whether a Realm's time maps to Git or version history, whether historical states are executable or only inspectable, whether an Avatar can safely enter a past state, and how knowledge learned later is handled there.

---

## Knowledge Realms

A future Knowledge Realms capability - a registry, portal or protocol - could let Realms declare their existence, publish entry requirements, advertise interfaces and capabilities, establish trust, and negotiate how Avatars enter and interoperate. Security, trust, authorisation and capability negotiation would be fundamental. Entering another Realm should never imply unrestricted transfer of local capability or authority, and security must precede any open federation.

One external check is outstanding: confirm the availability of a Knowledge Realms domain with an authoritative registrar before treating it as available or owned.
