# Pillars

## Overview

Pillars holds the internal knowledge of this island - the methodologies, frameworks, reference material, and domain knowledge owned and maintained here. It is the canonical source of truth for everything the island knows about itself and its subject areas.

---

## Aesthetics

[[Aesthetics/Aesthetics|Aesthetics]] holds the visual identity and design language of Knowledge Islands - the brand system, symbolic vocabulary, and asset library. It covers the visual style guide, the canonical symbol library, isometric tile sets for constructing territory maps, conceptual diagrams, and the logo.

---

## Philosophy

[[Philosophy/Philosophy|Philosophy]] is the portable Knowledge Islands model - the concepts, conventions, processes, activities, agents, and tools that define how any Knowledge Island operates. Arcadia, as the canonical seat of the Knowledge Islands system, holds the authoritative definition here; [[Knowledge Islands]] is its narrative entry point.

---

## Engineering Practice

[[Engineering Practice/Engineering Practice|Engineering Practice]] holds Arcadia's Techné engineering discipline: foundations, architecture, operating model, technology posture and diagrams. It is the canonical destination of the adopted source knowledge; [[Engineering Practice/MEMORY|Engineering Practice memory]] records scope and the remote-operation hold.

---

## Technē

[[Technē/Technē|Technē]] is a retained entry point for existing links into the discipline. Its [[Tool Ecosystem Map]] supplies current ownership signposting rather than a second engineering knowledge store.
